## App Description for Store Listing

Voice-Checkers is an accessible, voice-controlled checkers game designed for players of all abilities. Play traditional checkers using voice commands or standard touch/click controls. Perfect for individuals with visual impairments or anyone who wants a hands-free gaming experience.

## Key Features for Store Listing
