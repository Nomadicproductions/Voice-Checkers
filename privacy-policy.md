## Overview

Voice-Checkers ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our voice-controlled checkers game.

**Key Point: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

## Information We Do NOT Collect

Voice-Checkers is designed with privacy by default. We do not collect:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs  
- Location data
- Usage analytics or statistics
- Game progress or preferences
- Voice recordings or audio data
- IP addresses or browser information
- Cookies for tracking purposes

## How the App Works

Voice-Checkers operates entirely within your web browser:

- **Client-Side Only:** All game logic runs locally on your device
- **No Backend Servers:** We do not operate servers that receive your data
- **Local Processing:** Voice commands are processed by your browser's built-in speech recognition
- **No Data Transmission:** No game data or personal information leaves your device

## Browser Permissions

### Microphone Access
The App may request microphone permission to enable voice commands:

- Permission is handled entirely by your browser
- Voice processing occurs locally on your device  
- No audio data is transmitted to us or any third parties
- You can deny or revoke this permission at any time
- The game remains fully functional without microphone access

### Local Storage
The App does not use:
- Browser cookies for tracking
- Local storage for user data
- Session storage for personal information
- IndexedDB or other client-side databases

## Third-Party Services

Voice-Checkers uses only standard web technologies:

- **Web Speech API:** Browser-native speech recognition (no data sent to us)
- **Standard HTML5:** For game rendering and interaction
- **No External APIs:** No connections to advertising, analytics, or tracking services
- **No CDNs:** All resources are self-hosted

## Children's Privacy

Since we collect no personal information, Voice-Checkers is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any other age group.

## Data Security

While we collect no data, we maintain security best practices:
- All code runs client-side in your secure browser environment
- No server-side vulnerabilities as we operate no servers
- Regular updates to address any security issues in the client code

## Your Rights and Choices

Since no personal data is collected:
- No data exists to access, correct, or delete
- No opt-out procedures are needed for data collection
- You maintain full control over your browser's microphone permissions

## International Users

Voice-Checkers operates the same way globally:
- No data collection regardless of location
- No cross-border data transfers
- Compliance with privacy laws through data minimization (collecting nothing)

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
