Voice Recognition Details

How Voice Commands Work

Voice commands are processed using your browser's built-in Web Speech API. This means:

  • Local Processing: Initial processing happens in your browser
  • Cloud Services: Your browser may send audio to Apple/Google/Microsoft for transcription
  • No Storage: We do not store, record, or access any voice data
  • Optional Feature: Voice commands are entirely optional - you can play with touch/mouse only
