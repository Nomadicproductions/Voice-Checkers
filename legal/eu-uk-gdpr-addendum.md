# EU/UK GDPR Addendum

**Effective Date:** 26 September 2025  
**Scope:** European Union and United Kingdom Users  
**Regulation:** General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

## GDPR Compliance Statement

Voice-Checkers is fully compliant with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK Data Protection Act 2018 through a privacy-by-design approach that involves **no personal data processing**.

## Article 4 - Definitions

Under GDPR, "personal data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. Voice-Checkers processes **no personal data** as defined by GDPR.

## Data Processing Lawfulness (Article 6)

**No Legal Basis Required** - Since Voice-Checkers collects and processes no personal data, no legal basis under Article 6 is required or applied.

## Special Categories of Data (Article 9)

**No Special Category Data Processed** - Voice-Checkers does not process:
- Biometric data (voice patterns are not stored or analyzed)
- Health data
- Genetic data
- Any other special categories defined in Article 9

## Voice Recognition Processing

### Browser-Based Processing
- Voice commands are processed entirely by the user's browser
- No voice data is transmitted to Voice-Checkers developers
- Processing occurs under browser's privacy controls
- Users can deny or revoke microphone permissions

### Data Controller Relationship
- Voice-Checkers is not a data controller for voice processing
- Browser manufacturers control voice recognition processing
- Users have direct relationship with browser vendors for voice data

## Technical and Organizational Measures (Article 32)

While no personal data is processed, Voice-Checkers implements:

### Technical Measures
- Client-side only architecture prevents data collection
- No server-side storage or processing capabilities
- Standard web security practices
- Regular security updates

### Organizational Measures  
- Privacy-by-design development practices
- No employee access to user data (none exists)
- Open-source transparency
- Regular privacy impact assessments

## Data Protection Impact Assessment (Article 35)

**DPIA Status:** Not required - No personal data processing occurs, eliminating privacy risks to data subjects.

## Data Protection Officer (Articles 37-39)

**DPO Status:** Not required - No personal data processing and no monitoring of data subjects on a large scale.

## International Data Transfers (Chapter V)

**No International Transfers** - Since no personal data is collected or processed, no international data transfer mechanisms are required or utilized.

**Breach Notification Status:** Not applicable - No personal data exists to be breached.

## Consent Mechanisms (Article 7)
