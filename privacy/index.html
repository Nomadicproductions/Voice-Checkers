1. Overview

Kings Command ("the App") is designed with data minimization goals. This Policy explains what limited information may be processed when you use the App and your rights.

2. Summary (TL;DR)

  • No selling or "sharing" of personal data.
  • No persistent raw voice/audio storage (if voice features exist they are transient).
  • Gameplay / configuration data remains local unless a future sync feature is explicitly enabled by you.
  • Optional aggregate metrics may be used to improve performance and balance (no personal profiles).
  • Regional rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA-CPRA / Australia) are supported to the practical extent possible given minimal collection.

3. Data Categories

Not collected intentionally: user accounts, passwords, precise geolocation, advertising identifiers, payment data.

4. Purposes of Processing

  • Operate and enhance core gameplay.
  • Enable optional voice or accessibility features.
  • Improve performance & stability through aggregated diagnostics.
  • Inform balancing / AI heuristics using anonymized patterns.

5. Voice / Audio (If Present)

Any voice functionality relies on platform APIs or user-chosen third parties. Raw audio is not permanently stored by this project. External services you enable have their own privacy terms; review those separately.

6. Local Storage & Cookies

State is stored locally (e.g., localStorage). No advertising cookies. Any future analytics requiring consent will implement a notice and choice mechanism.

7. Sharing & Disclosure

  • Legal compliance (court or regulatory requests).
  • Security investigations or abuse mitigation.
  • Service providers strictly necessary for optional metrics or voice transcription (if implemented).

No selling or cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Security

We minimize data; ephemeral handling reduces exposure. Report potential issues to groundedfalse@gmail.com.

9. International Transfers

If optional services process data outside your region, they should rely on suitable safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses). Data minimization limits scope of transfers.

10. Your Rights

Depending on jurisdiction: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdraw consent, lodge complaints (EU/UK), opt-out (California). Low data retention may limit what can be produced.

11. Children

Not directed to children under 13 (or local equivalent). If you believe a child's data was provided, contact us for appropriate action.

12. Retention

Local data persists on your device until removal. Ephemeral data discarded post-processing. Aggregated metrics retained without personal identifiers for trend analysis.

13. Changes

Material updates will adjust the "Last Updated" date and may be summarized in a change log.
